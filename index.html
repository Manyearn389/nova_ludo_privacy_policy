1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Nova Ludo mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App", "Game", or "Service"), available under package name com.novaludo.game on the Google Play Store.

This policy informs you of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App. By downloading, accessing, or playing Nova Ludo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Information We Do NOT Collect

Nova Ludo is designed for instant play. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, email address, passwords, or payment card details.

B. Local Storage Data (On Your Device)

To preserve your progress and gameplay preferences, the App stores certain non-personal game data locally on your device (using standard browser localStorage or local app storage), including:

  • Game settings (Sound FX toggle, background music toggle, vibration haptics toggle, dark mode).
  • Virtual coin balance and in-game transaction history.
  • Unlocked cosmetic items (custom dice, boards, tokens, and VIP tier ranks).
  • Gameplay statistics (match wins, play streak, points, XP level, and capture counts).
  • Custom player nicknames and offline friend list entries.

This information remains exclusively on your device and is not transmitted to or stored on our external servers. You can erase this data at any time via the in-game Settings > Clear Cache & Reset Game Data button or by clearing the App's data in your Android device settings.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep Nova Ludo 100% free to play, the App uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). These services may collect certain device-level information to deliver advertisements (such as Banner ads, Fullscreen Interstitial ads, and Rewarded Video ads).

Information collected automatically by Google AdMob may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Android Advertising Identifier.
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
  • IP address (used to infer coarse location for localized ad delivery).
  • Ad interaction metrics (views, clicks, and completion of rewarded video ads).

4. Virtual In-Game Currency & Rewards Policy

Nova Ludo features a simulated virtual coin economy, Lucky Wheel spins, and cosmetic store:

  • All virtual coins (🪙), trophies (🏆), and VIP ranks are strictly in-game entertainment points.
  • Virtual coins have no real-world monetary cash value and cannot be redeemed, sold, traded, or exchanged for real fiat currency, cryptocurrency, gift cards, or real-world prizes.
  • Nova Ludo does not offer real-money gambling, cash prizes, deposits, or cash withdrawals.
  • Coins can be acquired solely through gameplay victories, daily reward calendars, lucky bonus wheel spins, or optional rewarded video ads.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Nova Ludo is designed for general audiences and family-friendly entertainment. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If a parent or guardian becomes aware that their child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

6. User Data Rights and Data Deletion

Because all gameplay data is stored strictly locally on your device, you possess complete control over your data at all times:

  • Delete In-Game Data: Open the game, tap the Settings (⚙️) icon, and select "Clear Cache & Reset Game Data".
  • Delete via Device Settings: On your Android device, go to Settings > Apps > Nova Ludo > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Nova Ludo permanently deletes all locally stored data, progress, and preferences from your device.

7. Security

We value your trust in using our Game. While no method of digital transmission or electronic storage is 100% impenetrable, we do not store sensitive personal information on remote servers, drastically minimizing security risks.
